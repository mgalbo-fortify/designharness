# Strategy — Applied AI

## Message hierarchy
1. **Promise:** AI, applied to the work your firm actually does.
2. **Mechanism:** We sit with your team to learn how the work really gets done, then design and build AI workflows inside the tools you already use. Your people stay in charge of every result.
3. **Reasons:** (a) we start from your work, not from a product: understand → design → build → run; (b) vendor-neutral and fixed-fee; (c) safe by design: business-grade tools, a person reviews every result; (d) a senior founder does the work.
4. **Proof:** the method shown on real-looking artifacts (week map, platform sheet, workflow, results), industry-specific examples, founder background.
5. **Objections:** start point, confidentiality, vendor lock-in, cost, losing the personal touch → assessment, standards, FAQ.
6. **Ask:** Book a free 20-minute call.
