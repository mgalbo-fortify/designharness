# Concept — Applied AI Associates

## Three directions

### A — "Drawn to spec"
Big idea: AI applied like an engineering drawing set: measured, specified, reviewed and signed off. The firm's method is a set of sheets.
Signature: a StickyStory of four drawing sheets (A-101 → A-104) whose diagrams draw themselves as you read each chapter.
Supporting: hero workflow traces itself step by step; red-pen review marks (the human check); hairline rules drawing in.
Type: Instrument Serif (display, italic accent) + Instrument Sans + IBM Plex Mono for annotations.
Palette: drafting paper, graphite ink, drafting blue for action, vermilion red pen only for human review.
Material: hairline drafting grid, title blocks, sheet numbers, dimension ticks. No shadows-on-everything; flat paper with precise lines.
Hero: left-aligned serif headline; right, a drawing sheet with a workflow being traced, ending in a red "Approved" mark.

### B — "The Briefing"
Big idea: the site is a partner-level memo on AI, marked up in the margins.
Signature: a long memo whose paragraphs get redlined and annotated as you scroll.
Type: Source Serif 4 + Inter. Palette: ivory, ink, oxblood.
Risk: text-heavy; little product to show; reads like a law firm, not a builder.

### C — "Signal"
Big idea: noise to signal: scattered tasks resolve into one clean line.
Signature: a pinned scroll scene where a cloud of dots organizes into a workflow.
Type: Geist + Geist Mono. Palette: near-black, cool gray, one electric accent.
Risk: abstract "AI" imagery is exactly the cliché the audience distrusts; dark tech look reads startup, not advisor.

## Chosen: A, "Drawn to spec"
Why: "Applied" is an engineering word, and the drawing-set language (specified, measured, reviewed, signed off) answers this audience's real fear, which is AI done carelessly. It speaks natively to the architects, engineers and manufacturers on the target list while reading as rigorous to law and finance, and the red pen turns "a person reviews every result" into something you can see.

### Type
Display Instrument Serif 400 (+ italic) at up to 96px, −0.02em, line-height 0.98. Text Instrument Sans 400–600. Mono IBM Plex Mono 400/500 for sheet numbers, annotations, data.

### Motion vocabulary
- **Signature:** the Method StickyStory. Pinned drawing sheet changes per chapter: week-map bars extend, platform rows resolve with verdict chips, workflow nodes connect, before/after figures count. Each sheet gets a red-pen mark when "reviewed".
- **Supporting:** (1) hero workflow trace: steps appear in order along a drawing line (data-sequence) with connector rules drawing, ending in a red circle "Approved" mark; (2) red-pen strikes on the tension symptoms; (3) rules drawing in under section heads.
- **Defaults:** split lines on headlines; reveal "up" on blocks; artifacts "rise".
- **Ambient:** none. The grid is static. Calm is the point.
