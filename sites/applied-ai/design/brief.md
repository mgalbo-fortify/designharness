# Brief — Applied AI Associates

## Snapshot
- **What it is:** A founder-led AI adoption and development firm. Helps firms understand AI, recommends the right platforms, builds custom workflows inside the tools they already use, trains their people, and keeps it running.
- **Site type:** single landing page, B2B services (agency arc)
- **Pages:** `/` (+ 404, styleguide)
- **Primary action:** "Book a free 20-minute call" → form on the page (`#contact`)
- **Secondary action:** "See how we engage" → `#engage`

## Audience
- **Who:** owners, managing partners, COOs and operations leads at 10–200 person firms in Fairfield County and the NY metro. Professional services first (law, wealth management, private equity, architecture and engineering), plus manufacturers, contractors, consumer brands and real estate.
- **Trigger:** clients and competitors are talking about AI; staff are already pasting work into public chatbots; a software vendor is pushing an AI add-on; a partner asks "what's our AI plan?"
- **What they want:** a credible, senior advisor who tells them plainly where AI fits, what to buy (and not buy), and builds one thing that actually works without putting client data at risk.
- **Sophistication:** aware, skeptical, short on time. Allergic to hype.
- **Top objections:**
  1. "We don't know where to start." → the AI Assessment
  2. "Our client data is confidential." → business-grade tools you control; a person reviews every result
  3. "We'll get sold software we don't need." → vendor-neutral; start with what you own; subscriptions not marked up
  4. "Consultants bill forever." → fixed fees agreed in writing; no hourly billing
  5. "It'll make us less personal." → clients still deal with your people

## Brand
- **Existing assets:** none for Applied AI Associates.
- **Personality:** precise, senior, plain-spoken.
- **Must avoid:** Many Hands' playfulness (illustrated characters, pastel storefronts), AI clichés (glowing brains, neural nets, robots, purple gradients, sparkles).

## Assumptions
- The light-touch entry offer is an "AI Assessment" (fixed fee, about two weeks, written roadmap). Alternatives are listed in the hand-off for the user to choose.
- Contact is a form on the page; no scheduling link yet.
- Founder named "Mike" (first name only until the user supplies a full name and photo).
- Domain placeholder `appliedaiassociates.example`.
- Workflow examples and all figures inside product artifacts are illustrative and labelled as such.
