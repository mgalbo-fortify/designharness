# Strategy — Nightshift

## Message hierarchy
1. **Promise:** Cybersecurity that works the night shift. (Someone is watching when you aren't.)
2. **Mechanism:** Analysts watch devices, email and logins around the clock and stop attacks before they become incidents. No security team required.
3. **Reasons:** (a) they act, not just alert: contained in a median 18 minutes; (b) it covers the three ways in (devices, email, logins); (c) you understand it: plain-English reports, insurance evidence.
4. **Proof:** the night scenario (mechanism shown), facts (24/7/365, 18 min, 30 min setup, $12), the report artifact.
5. **Objections:** cost, no IT staff, alert fatigue, contract, performance → FAQ + microcopy.
6. **Ask:** Get a free security check.
