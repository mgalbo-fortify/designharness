# Sample brief: Nightshift (fictional company, for demonstrating the harness)

## What the site has to do
Landing page for Nightshift, a managed security service (managed detection and
response) for businesses with 10–200 employees that don't have a security team.
Goal: get owners / IT leads to book a free security check (a 20-minute call plus
a scan of their Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace). Traffic: LinkedIn ads,
referrals from IT providers, and search.

Audience: owners, COOs and the one "IT person" at small and mid-sized
businesses: accounting firms, clinics, law offices, manufacturers. They know
they're a target, they've heard of ransomware, they can't hire a security
team, and they're tired of tools that send alerts nobody reads.

## Initial copy

Headline: Cybersecurity that works the night shift.
Subhead: Nightshift's analysts watch your devices, email and logins around the
clock, and stop attacks before they become incidents. No security team required.
CTA: Get a free security check

How it works
1. Connect: 30-minute setup. No hardware.
2. We watch: analysts and automation monitor every device, inbox and login 24/7.
3. We act: we contain threats immediately and explain what happened in plain English.

What's covered
- Endpoint detection and response on every laptop and server
- Email protection for Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace
- Identity monitoring: impossible travel, MFA fatigue, risky sign-ins
- Incident response included, not billed by the hour
- Monthly report your leadership will actually read
- Evidence for cyber insurance and compliance questionnaires

Facts we can use
- 24/7/365 monitoring by a US-based security operations center
- Median time to contain a threat: 18 minutes (2025 customer data)
- Setup takes about 30 minutes
- From $12 per user per month
- Month-to-month after a 3-month start

FAQ
- Do we need IT staff to use Nightshift? No. We work with whoever manages your computers today, including outside IT providers.
- What happens when you find something? We contain it first (isolate the device, lock the account), then call you and explain.
- Will it slow our computers down? The agent is lightweight; most people never notice it.
- Does it help with cyber insurance? Yes. We provide the evidence insurers ask for (EDR, MFA, monitoring).
- Is there a long contract? Three months to start, then month to month.

Form fields: name, work email, company, number of employees.
No testimonials or customer logos yet. Brand: none yet, open to direction.
